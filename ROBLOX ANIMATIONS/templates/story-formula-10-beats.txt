THE 10-BEAT STORY FORMULA (~2-minute vertical Short, ~410 words, ~3.3 words/second)

1. WORLD HOOK (0–5 s)
   One magical rule of the world.
   Opens with: "In this world, ..."

2. WONDER MONTAGE (5–15 s)
   3 quick, funny, visual examples of the rule.
   Opens with: "You see, ..."

3. THE RULE (15–20 s)
   One law nobody breaks.
   Opens with: "And there's only one rule."

4. OMEN (20–25 s)
   A strange detail that becomes the twist later.
   Opens with: "But here's the weird part."

5. UNDERDOG INTRO (25–45 s)
   The hero has the weakest power and a mean nickname. A bully has the best power.
   Opens with: "My name is [NAME]."

6. WISDOM LINE (~45 s)
   An elder's saying that unlocks the ending.
   Opens with: "[Grandma] always says one thing."

7. DISASTER (50–75 s)
   Big set-piece. Every strong kid fails, one by one.
   Short punchy lines, onomatopoeia.

8. REALISATION (~75 s)
   The twist explains the omen.
   Opens with: "That's when I understood."

9. UNDERDOG WINS (75–105 s)
   The "useless" power is the only one that works. The hero saves the bully.
   Repeat the wisdom line.

10. WARM BUTTON (last 10 s)
   Status flipped, callback joke.
   Opens with: "Now ..."

STYLE RULES
- Present-tense energy, short sentences.
- Concrete numbers ("a hundred years", "a thousand lanterns").
- At most 1–2 lines of dialogue.
- Kid-safe, emotional but hopeful.
- Pick a world rule that is VISUAL and has a BUILT-IN WEAKNESS.

TITLE
3–6 words + one emoji. Example: "Follow The Little Light 🏮"

SHOT-BY-SHOT QA CHECKLIST

THE LOOP
[ ] Render 3 stills per shot (start, middle, end) at 25–30%
[ ] Put them on contact sheets (6–8 shots × 3 frames per image)
[ ] Look at EVERY frame yourself
[ ] For each problem: root cause in the script → fix at the source → rebuild → re-render only those shots
[ ] New low-res preview to check motion between stills
[ ] Repeat until every shot passes

CHECK EVERY SHOT FOR
[ ] Everyone who should be in the shot is visible for the whole shot
[ ] We see faces, not the backs of heads
[ ] Feet are on the ground / deck, not inside it or floating
[ ] The camera is not inside a character, a prop or under the floor
[ ] Characters riding something move WITH it
[ ] Every close-up shows the character, not an object floating alone
[ ] The face expression matches the line
[ ] The cut lands on the right word
[ ] Fog/smoke is visible, with no checkerboard pattern
[ ] Nothing slides or pops at the cut

COMMON BUGS → FIXES
Characters vanish mid-shot        → a key on the cut frame: end every key at shot end − 1
Character invisible               → check Head Type (3 = Woman Head) and props in the way
Backs of heads                    → place the camera on the side the character faces (+Y rig)
Feet inside the floor             → ground() helper: use the real floor height
Rider slides off what it rides    → use the same keyframe type as the vehicle
Object floating alone in frame    → make it a two-shot with the character
Camera inside geometry            → re-stage, re-path, remove props in the way
Fog/smoke invisible or dark       → more density + a soft self-glow
Checkerboard fog                  → volume tile 4 for previews, 8 for 1080p
Camera spins 360° between keys    → compatible Euler conversion
Motion smears into the next shot  → motion-blur shutter starts on the frame

BEFORE A BIG LOOK CHANGE
[ ] Save the .blend
[ ] Render 5–10 stills of the new look and approve them first
